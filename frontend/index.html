Built on real statistics, not guesses

Many "AI analytics" tools let a language model improvise numbers. NexlyticX computes every figure with pandas and SciPy on your full dataset, and only then phrases it in plain language.

  • Hypothesis tests show their p-value; plain calculations are labelled "Exact calculation".
  • Hidden problems, like zeros standing in for missing values, are caught and explained.
  • A single serious issue stops a dataset from being graded "Good", however high the average.
  • Every score and finding can be traced back to the data behind it.

Your data stays yours

Plain answers to the questions people ask before uploading a file.

Guest sessions are temporary

Without an account, files are held in server memory only, never written to disk, and deleted after a period of inactivity (2 hours by default).

Accounts keep what you choose

When you are signed in, your datasets are saved to your private storage so you can return to them. Delete any dataset, or your entire account, at any time.

No tracking, no third parties

No analytics scripts, no ads and no external fonts. Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes, never in plain text.
